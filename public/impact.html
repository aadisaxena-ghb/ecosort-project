The Regulatory Shift

Rules are catching up to the crisis

📜

Solid Waste Management Rules, 2026

India's Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change framework mandates four-stream segregation (wet, dry, sanitary, hazardous) for every generator.

🏢

Bulk generator accountability

Apartment complexes, universities, hospitals, and tech parks must process organic waste on-site, shifting responsibility directly to generators.
