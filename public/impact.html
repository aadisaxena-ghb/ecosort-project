The Regulatory Shift

Rules are catching up to the problem

Solid Waste Management Rules, 2026

India's Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change replaced the 2016 framework with rules mandating four-stream segregation (wet, dry, sanitary, hazardous) for every waste generator, effective April 2026.

Bulk generator accountability

Large residential complexes, hospitals, and campuses above set thresholds must now process organic waste on-site or obtain certification — shifting responsibility upstream, closer to where waste is created.

Where tools like this fit

As accountability moves to individual generators, a fast, correct answer at the point of disposal becomes more valuable — that's the exact gap EcoSort AI is built to close.
