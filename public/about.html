About the Project

Why EcoSort AI Exists

Most people don't mismanage waste on purpose — they just lack an instant, reliable way to check where an unusual item belongs. EcoSort AI closes that gap at the exact moment it matters: right before something is discarded.

Guiding Principles

Responsible AI, by design

⚖️ Fairness

The retriever matches plain-language phrasing and slang rather than rigid taxonomy, so varied user descriptions receive accurate guidance.

🔍 Transparency

Every answer provides clear reasoning referencing the underlying rule, preventing unexplained "black box" decisions.

🛡️ Safety & Ethics

Identifies toxic, hazardous, and electronic waste explicitly to protect sanitation workers and ecosystems from chemical exposure.

🔒 Privacy First

Only the item description or image is analyzed. No personal data is stored, tracked, or sold.

IBM SkillsBuild · Innovation Tool

Incorporation of IBM BOB

💡 Ideation & Problem Framing with IBM BOB

Utilized IBM BOB during Stage 1 (Empathize) and Stage 2 (Define) of Design Thinking to model user personas, map citizen disposal pain points, and define the core "How Might We" problem statement.

⚙️ RAG Pipeline & Knowledge Curation

Leveraged IBM BOB to synthesize complex municipal solid-waste frameworks (CPCB & SWM Rules 2026) into a lightweight, retrieval-optimized JSON knowledge taxonomy with practical habit tips.
